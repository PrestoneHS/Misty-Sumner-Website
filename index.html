Step by Step

The Path to Your Keys

Knowing each milestone makes the whole experience far less stressful. Here's exactly what to expect from your first call to closing day.

1
Pre-Approval
Speak with a lender, gather documents, and receive your official pre-approval letter.
2
Home Search
Tour homes in your price range, refine your criteria, and find the right fit.
3
Offer & Negotiation
Submit a strategic offer, negotiate terms, and sign the purchase agreement.
4
Due Diligence
Inspection, appraisal, title exam, and finalizing your loan — all handled.
5
Pre-Closing
Confirm repairs, conduct final walk-through, wire closing funds, review disclosure.
6
Closing Day 🎉
Sign the documents, receive your keys — you are officially a homeowner.
